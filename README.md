# ⚡ Electric Vehicle (EV) Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence and data analytics project evaluating Electric Vehicle (EV) sales patterns, manufacturer market shares, state-wise adoption trends, and growth metrics. 

This repository contains the data models, analytical documentation, raw datasets, and an interactive **Power BI** dashboard (`.pbix`).

---

## 📌 Project Overview

With the rapid expansion of clean mobility, understanding EV penetration across regions and vehicle classes is crucial for automotive makers, policymakers, and infrastructure planners. 

This project explores:
- **Market Penetration & Trends:** Tracking EV adoption rates against internal combustion vehicles over time.
- **State-Wise Breakdown:** Identifying top-performing states and regional adoption disparities across India.
- **Manufacturer Performance:** Evaluating major 2-wheeler, 3-wheeler, and 4-wheeler EV makers and their market shares.
- **Key Business Inquiries:** Addressing targeted primary and secondary analytical business questions regarding CAGR, top makers, and state-level growth.
